02 The Ucluelet coverage report a diagnostic detour
  • Bug report: “no buses on the Island”

    A user in Ucluelet, on Vancouver Island, opened the app and saw nothing.

  • Diagnose — not a bug, a boundary

    BusPulse Vancouver rides TransLink, which is Metro Vancouver only. The nearest TransLink stop to Ucluelet is ~162 km away; the Island is BC Transit territory.

    ~162 km to nearest stop8,731 stops audited
  • Fix the misleading empty state

    Instead of “no stops within 500 m,” the app now checks the nearest stop and, past a 10 km threshold, says you’re outside the coverage area — in English and Punjabi.

    COVERAGE_MAX_MnearestStopDistance()outOfCoverage
  • Commit & push

    The honest coverage message ships in code — waiting for the next binary to carry it to users.

How it’s wired

Why the API key isn’t in the app

The one piece of the build worth a picture: 1.1 ships without the TransLink key embedded. The phone talks to a caching proxy that holds the key server-side, so nothing sensitive rides in the binary or in the public repo.

What’s left, and what to watch

When review clears

  • Apple emails a decision — usually 24–48 h.
  • On approval, 1.1 auto-releases to everyone.
  • Nothing to click; the release is automatic.

Watch-outs

  • Don’t make the repo private on a free plan — it kills the Pages privacy/support URLs Apple requires.
  • Builds expire in 30 days — don’t let the next one sit unsubmitted.
  • Do a one-time visual check of Punjabi tab labels in Expo Go.
