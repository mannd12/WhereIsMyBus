# BusPulse Vancouver — Shorts production kit (IG Reels · TikTok · YouTube Shorts)

_Drafted 2026-10-14. Organic only. Screen-recorded app + ElevenLabs voice. Every short ends on
`shorts-endcard.png` (1080×1920, bilingual, QR)._

## Production recipe (same for every short)

1. **Record the app** on your iPhone: Control Centre → Screen Record. Put the phone in Punjabi
   (ਸੈਟਿੰਗਾਂ → ਭਾਸ਼ਾ → ਪੰਜਾਬੀ) for the Punjabi cut, English for the English cut. Record each
   action slowly — you'll speed it up 1.5–2× in the edit. Use a real stop you ride (e.g. a stop
   on the R1 / 319 / 321) so the arrivals look alive.
2. **Voice:** ElevenLabs → *Eleven Multilingual v2* (or v3). Pick a warm voice; **speed 0.95**,
   stability ~0.5. Paste the Punjabi VO lines below *in Gurmukhi* (not transliterated) — it reads
   them correctly. Listen once: if a number sounds off, write it as a word (ਦੋ, ਪੰਜ, ਦਸ).
   Generate English from the EN lines with the same voice for consistency.
3. **Assemble in CapCut** (phone): 9:16 · 1080×1920 · 30 fps. Order: hook text (0–1.5 s) →
   recording → end card (last 2.5 s). Add **auto-captions in the spoken language** — most
   people watch muted. Trending low-volume audio under the VO is fine.
4. **Export** 1080p, under 60 s. Post the same file to all three platforms; swap captions only.
5. **Two cuts per short:** Punjabi (app in Punjabi + Punjabi VO) and English (app in English +
   English VO). Punjabi cut first on IG/FB (family-share audience), English first on TikTok/YT.

**Rules:** say *iPhone*. Never show Abbotsford/Chilliwack. Don't show your location name if you
don't want to — pick a busy stop like Surrey Central.

**Best posting window:** 6–9 pm, Tue–Sun. One short every 2–3 days; re-post the best one after
two weeks. Pin "The Flip" on every profile.
