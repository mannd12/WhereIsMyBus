# BusPulse Vancouver — Punjabi launch posts

_Drafted 2026-09-23. 1.1.0 (Punjabi) is live on the App Store since Aug 27._
_App link: https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/id6782258055_

**Claim check (2026-09-23):** of 196 Vancouver-transit apps on the Canadian App Store,
only BusPulse Vancouver lists Punjabi as a supported language (the other two hits — PRESTO and
MetrO — aren't Vancouver bus apps). So "the only TransLink bus app in Punjabi" is a
checkable claim. Avoid "first" — that's harder to prove.

**Two rules for every post:**
- Say **iPhone** — the app is iOS-only. Android relatives will ask.
- Post in **Surrey / Delta / Vancouver** groups only. **Not Abbotsford** — it's BC Transit
  territory; those downloads hit the out-of-coverage message and become 1-star reviews.
