## Email

**Subject:** Surrey-area developer builds the only Punjabi-language bus app for Metro Vancouver

Hi [producer name / RED FM newsroom],

I'm Prabhdeep Mann, a local developer. I built **BusPulse Vancouver**, a free iPhone app that shows live TransLink bus arrivals for every stop in Metro Vancouver — and last month I released it fully in Punjabi (ਪੰਜਾਬੀ).

As far as I can find, it's the **only TransLink bus app on the App Store that works in Punjabi**. I built it for the parents and grandparents in our community who can take the bus on their own if the phone just tells them, in their own language, when it's coming.

What it does:
- Live, second-by-second bus arrivals for every stop
- Your bus moving on the map, so you can see it coming
- A reminder notification before it arrives
- Free, no account, nothing collected — the whole app is in Punjabi with one tap

I'd love to do a short segment (Punjabi or English) on why I built it and how families can set it up on an older relative's phone in under a minute. Happy to come in or do it by phone.

1. **ਸਮੱਸਿਆ:** ਸਾਡੇ ਬਜ਼ੁਰਗ ਬੱਸ ਲੈਣ ਤੋਂ ਡਰਦੇ ਹਨ — ਬੱਸ ਕਦੋਂ ਆਵੇਗੀ, ਕਿਹੜੀ ਬੱਸ, ਸਭ ਕੁਝ ਅੰਗਰੇਜ਼ੀ ਵਿੱਚ।

## Likely questions — have answers ready

- **Android?** Not yet — iPhone first. (Be honest; it's the #1 question you'll get.)
- **Abbotsford / Chilliwack?** No — BC Transit, different system. Say "Metro Vancouver" every time.
- **How is it different from the TransLink/Transit app?** It's in Punjabi, and it's simpler: no trip-planning maze, just "when is my bus coming."
- **Who translated it?** You did, as a Punjabi speaker, and you're taking corrections from the community — invite listeners to send word fixes. That's a great on-air moment.
