# Sticker — 3" die-cut circle

Files: `sticker-circle-punjabi.*` and `sticker-circle-english.*` — `.html` (source) · `.pdf` (print file, 3.125" × 3.125" with 1/16" bleed) · `.png` (1200 px preview). Same design; order both.
1/16" bleed on every side) · `sticker-circle-punjabi.png` (1200 px preview).

**Ordering (either is fine):**
- **StickerYou** (Canadian, ships from Toronto) — Die-Cut Stickers → Circle → 3" → upload the PDF.
- **Sticker Mule** — Die cut stickers → 3"×3" → upload the PDF; their proof will show the cut line.
- Pick **glossy vinyl** (survives phone cases, rain, dishwashers). ~100 pcs ≈ $60–80 CAD.
- Approve the proof only if the QR is fully inside the cut line and nothing important is in
  the outer 1/8".

**Where they go (legal):** hand-outs at gurdwara langar, student-association tables, Punjabi
shops *with permission*, inside the poster envelope you give a shop owner. **Not** on bus stops,
shelters, poles or SkyTrain property — TransLink/city bylaw, removed in days, and it would be
traced back to the "community app."

**Test before ordering:** print one on plain paper at 100% and scan it from 30 cm with your phone.
